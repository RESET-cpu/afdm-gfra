## Evidence that can support an analytical narrative

The reported results compare complete configured receivers. The local STMP baseline already uses a classifier (`src/configs/final/STMP.json`, `activity_evidence="classifier"`), so neither DRE nor the presence of a learned generative prior independently explains the proposed-versus-STMP gain. Covariance-conditioned mean training, variance estimation/adjustment, and damping differ. Chapter III's joint-moment equations explain why channel and activity estimates exchange information; joint improvement is consistent with that design, but the curves alone do not causally attribute the gain to one module.

## Model details that make interpretation precise

- Activity is sampled conditional on a nonempty active set, so expected active count is Kp/[1−(1−p)^K]. In the K sweep it is approximately 2.28, 4.06, 6.01, 8.00, 10.00. In the p sweep it is approximately 2.29, 4.06, 6.01, 8.00, 10.00. Similar loads in the two sweeps do not make them replicated experiments: K and the candidate dimension differ, and damping differs in the high-K cases.
- `src/evaluation/profiles.py` defines A/B/C/D and matches total **expected** tap power to D without per-realization normalization. Geometry, activity, pilots, and standardized noise are paired; A/B/C fading streams differ, and D retains its original stream. No retraining is performed across profiles. Common direction and satellite Doppler are retained; this is a profile-mismatch test, not a complete time-varying Doppler-spectrum test.
- Denoising PLOT_DATA states that the colored and AWGN learned networks use bitwise-identical historical cloud truth/query/standard-noise hashes. Imported sparse baselines share scene definitions and standard-noise hashes but have cross-platform truth/query hash differences. Existing main-text statement of identical channels/noise is specifically about the **two neural estimators**, and should remain scoped that way.
- The genie curve is analytic conditional MSE given true path geometry, powers, and LOS complex gain. It is a reference with extra information, not an attainable estimator under the receiver's observation model.

## Move protocol out of result paragraphs without hiding it

Suggested structure: keep one short paragraph in VI-A describing paired scene counts, retrospective evaluation/overlap, retention of all finite outputs, and a direct pointer to a new reproducibility appendix. Keep one brief user-count reference to the retrospective schedule near that figure. The appendix holds the exact schedule, diagnostic selection, and failure counts below. This improves reading flow without erasing qualifications.

Material facts that must survive relocation:

1. STMP defaults to weight 0.2; proposed defaults to 0.4; both use 60–600 iterations and unchanged native stopping criteria. Local STMP uses a classifier. The comparison is between complete receivers, not an isolated colored-training ablation.
2. SNR uses 1,000 scenes per point; pilot uses first 500 of the planned 1,000; user/activity/cross use first 500; scenes are paired and reused. Development scenes and baseline-threshold development overlap evaluations; adopted rules are reused without refitting. The SNR range −4…6 dB was narrowed after interim inspection, with old 8/10 dB data archived (metadata fact currently absent from main prose; at least preserve in the audit/protocol record).
3. User-count STMP weight 0.2 at K=20,40 and 0.1 at K=60,80,100. It was selected after inspecting original 500-scene results and a 12-input diagnostic. All 12 inputs are included in plotted panels. Original weight-0.2 failures at K=60,80,100 were 498,479,500/500; outputs remain archived. Current cap counts are 0,0,0,3,14 at K=20,40,60,80,100; all 17 finite outputs remain.
4. Proposed user-count weight 0.2 for the full K=100 panel after preliminary inspection and for only K=80 scene 372 after that original run hit the cap; all other K=80 scenes retain 0.4. This is a selected single-scene replacement, not a full-panel weight-0.2 experiment. It must remain explicitly disclosed in the appendix, not collapsed into “parameter tuning.”
5. Activity sweep uses unchanged weights 0.2/0.4. All 2,500 proposed runs converge; STMP has one cap output at each p=0.20,0.25, both retained. Nonempty activity conditioning and nominal p axis are stated once in VI-A.
6. Cross-profile sweep uses unchanged weights and stopping rules. All 12,000 outputs of each receiver converge. D outputs are reused after input/config checks. Retrospective overlap and fixed-profile scope remain stated once with a pointer, not repeated in every results paragraph.
7. SNR −4 dB has proposed 3/1,000 and STMP 1/1,000 capped runs. Pilot Q=8 has proposed 2/500 and STMP 462/500 capped runs. These facts are in saved source summaries and should not be concealed by a broad assertion that the proposed method always converges.
