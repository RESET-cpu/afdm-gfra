\section{Activity Sampling in the Simulations}
\label{app:activity-sampling}

Independent Bernoulli activity vectors with parameter $p$ are
conditioned on at least one active device. The resulting marginal
probability is $p_{\rm eff}=p/[1-(1-p)^K]$.
In the simulations, the receiver uses $p_{\rm eff}$ in place of $p$
in its activity-prior-dependent updates, retaining a factorized
Bernoulli approximation to the conditional joint distribution.
