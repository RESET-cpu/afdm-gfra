\section{Phase Estimation}
\label{app:frontend}
\label{app:phase-estimation}

When phases are unavailable, stack the transposed frame observations as
$\mathbf Y_{\rm s}=[\mathbf Y_0,\ldots,\mathbf Y_{Q-1}]^{\mathsf T}
\in\mathbb C^{QN\times M}$. Approximate each device by one dominant path.
Absorbing the common scalar phase
into the gain gives
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathbf G_0(d,\nu)&=e^{-\mathrm j2\pi c_1d^2}\mathbf G(d,\nu;0),\\
 [\mathbf h_k^{\rm p}(d,\nu)]_{qN+r}
 &=e^{\mathrm j2\pi\nu qT_{\rm frame}}
 [\mathbf G_0(d,\nu)\mathbf p_{qk}]_r.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{frontend:eq:phase-atom}
\end{equation}
Search $d\in[0,N)$ and $|\nu|\leq\nu_{\max}$, using the channel model's
Doppler bound. Since $2Nc_1\in\mathbb N_+$,
adding integer multiples of $N$ to $d$ changes the operator only by a
scalar phase absorbed by the gains.

With $\mathbf h_k^{\rm p}=\mathbf h_k^{\rm p}(d_k,\nu_k)$, use
$\mathbf Y_{\rm s}\approx\sum_k\mathbf h_k^{\rm p}\mathbf b_k^{\mathsf T}
+\mathbf W_{\rm s}$ and independent gain priors
$\mathbf b_k\sim\CN(\mathbf0,\gamma_k\I_M)$, $\gamma_k\geq0$.
Integrating out the gains under this working approximation yields
the Gaussian marginal-likelihood objective
\cite[Sec.~2.1]{tipping2003}, extended here to complex observations with
$M$ antenna columns:
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathbf S&=\widehat\sigma^2\I_{QN}
 +\sum_k\gamma_k\mathbf h_k^{\rm p}\mathbf h_k^{{\rm p},\mathsf H},\\
 \mathcal L&=\log\det\mathbf S+
 \frac1M\tr(\mathbf Y_{\rm s}^{\mathsf H}\mathbf S^{-1}\mathbf Y_{\rm s}).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{frontend:eq:marginal-objective}
\end{equation}
Use $\widehat\sigma^2$ from \eqref{eq:noise-estimation} and minimize
$\mathcal L$ by coordinate updates. For device $k$, fix the others and define
$\mathbf W_k=(\widehat\sigma^2\I_{QN}
+\sum_{j\ne k}\gamma_j\mathbf h_j^{\rm p}\mathbf h_j^{{\rm p},\mathsf H})^{-1}$,
$s_k=\mathbf h_k^{{\rm p},\mathsf H}\mathbf W_k\mathbf h_k^{\rm p}$, and
$t_k=\|\mathbf h_k^{{\rm p},\mathsf H}\mathbf W_k\mathbf Y_{\rm s}\|^2$.
For a given $(d_k,\nu_k)$, the minimizing power is
\begin{equation}
 \gamma_k^\star=\max\left\{0,\frac{t_k/(Ms_k)-1}{s_k}\right\}.
 \label{frontend:eq:marginal-power}
\end{equation}
With \eqref{frontend:eq:marginal-power}, maximizing $t_k/s_k$ minimizes
the coordinate objective.

Pilot correlations on $128$ phase bins provide three coarse candidates.
Doppler shifts yielding the same frame phase $\beta$ are
$\nu_j=(\beta+2\pi j)/(2\pi T_{\rm frame})$, $j\in\mathbb Z$,
within the search range. Rank these candidates by $t_k/s_k$ and refine
the best $12$ using L-BFGS-B \cite{zhu1997}, with at most $25$ local
iterations per candidate within a differentiable interval of the waveform.
Initialize powers to zero and perform two sweeps in descending order of
initial pilot-correlation scores. Retaining the current parameters among
the candidates ensures a non-increasing objective up to numerical tolerance.

The estimated Doppler gives $\widehat\beta_k$ and $\widehat{\mathbf A}$
through \eqref{eq:twc-frame-channel}--\eqref{eq:effective-pilot}.
Pass only the phases to Algorithm~\ref{alg:gp-mp-ccd} and keep them fixed
during MP. The receiver uses the learned prior in Section~\ref{sec:learning};
the single-path approximation is confined to phase estimation.
