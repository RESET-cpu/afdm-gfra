\section{AFDM Channel Matrix: Internal Derivation}

The chirp-periodic extension over the prefix is
\begin{equation}
 s_{qk}[t]=e^{\mathrm j2\pi c_1(t^2-\langle t\rangle_N^2)}
 s_{qk}[\langle t\rangle_N],\qquad -N_{\rm CPP}\leq t<0,
 \label{eq:cpp}
\end{equation}
where $\langle x\rangle_N=x-N\lfloor x/N\rfloor$ and
$2Nc_1\in\mathbb N_+$. This relation is incorporated into the
continuous waveform defined in the manuscript.

Substituting the basis waveform in \eqref{eq:continuous-waveform}
into \eqref{eq:path-operator-system} gives
\begin{equation}
 \begin{split}
 [\mathbf G(d,\nu;g)]_{rn}
 &=\frac{e^{\mathrm j2\pi[c_1d^2+c_2(n^2-r^2)-nd/N+\nu T_sg]}}{N}\\
 &\quad\times\sum_{t=0}^{N-1}
 e^{\mathrm j2\pi[(n-r)/N-2c_1d+\nu T_s]t}
 e^{\mathrm j2\pi\zeta_{tn}d},
 \end{split}
 \label{eq:path-entry-expansion}
\end{equation}
where $\zeta_{tn}=\zeta_n(\langle t-d\rangle_N)$.
Factors with phases equal to integer multiples of $2\pi$ are one because $t,\zeta_{tn}$ are integers
and $w_{t-d}-(t-d)$ is an integer multiple of $N$.
For integer $d$, the final factor is one, giving

\begin{align}
 \mathbf G(d,\nu;g)
 &=\mathbf F_{\rm A}\mathbf D_{\nu,g}\bm\Omega_d
   \bm\Pi_{d\bmod N}\mathbf F_{\rm A}^{\mathsf H},
 \label{eq:integer-operator}\\
 [\bm\Omega_d]_{tt}
 &=e^{\mathrm j2\pi c_1[(t-d)^2-\langle t-d\rangle_N^2]},
 \label{eq:integer-prefix-phase}
\end{align}
where $[\bm\Pi_l\mathbf s]_t=s[\langle t-l\rangle_N]$.
The value $d\bmod N$ determines the cyclic shift, whereas the
full delay determines the CPP phase. The same expression therefore
covers delays exceeding $N$ samples.

For the reference frame, the useful part starts at
$g_0=N_{\rm CPP}$. Combining the path responses in
\eqref{eq:path-operator-system} with the path gains and array response
gives the effective channel in \eqref{eq:channel}.
In frame $q$, the starting sample is
$g_q=N_{\rm CPP}+q(N+N_{\rm CPP})$, and
\[
 \mathbf D_{\nu_k,g_q}
 =e^{\mathrm j2\pi\nu_kqT_{\rm frame}}\mathbf D_{\nu_k,N_{\rm CPP}}.
\]
Since this scalar factor is common to all paths of device $k$,
the device matrix satisfies
$\mathbf H_k^{(q)}=e^{\mathrm jq\beta_k}\mathbf H_k$, establishing
\eqref{eq:twc-frame-channel}.
