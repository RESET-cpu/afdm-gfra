### English Abstract

This paper investigates joint activity detection and channel estimation (JADCE) for grant-free random access in low Earth orbit satellite Internet-of-Things systems without global navigation satellite system (GNSS) assistance. We develop a multi-frame affine frequency division multiplexing (AFDM) transmission scheme to handle large differential delays and Doppler shifts at the satellite receiver, and derive its input--output relationship. Under a blockwise constant channel model, separating inter-frame Doppler phase rotations provides multiple observations of a shared effective channel. To account for fractional delay--Doppler leakage, a score-based generative prior is learned directly on multiantenna AFDM effective channels without imposing sparse or grid-aligned channel assumptions. A turbo message-passing receiver incorporates this prior by jointly denoising each user's complete channel tensor and exchanging extrinsic information with a linear estimator. The channel denoiser is pretrained with colored Gaussian perturbations and conditioned on their covariance. An activity classifier is trained using the denoiser's conditional-mean features, while conditional-mean derivatives provide channel variance estimates. The resulting receiver, denoted C-STMP, retains scalar-variance messages and updates the query variance using posterior expected residual energy. Simulations with known inter-frame phases show lower channel estimation errors and activity detection error rates than the reference score-based turbo message-passing receiver across the evaluated signal-to-noise ratios.

### Chinese Abstract

本文研究无全球导航卫星系统（GNSS）辅助的低轨卫星物联网免授权随机接入中的联合活动检测与信道估计（JADCE）问题。针对大差分时延与多普勒频移，提出多帧仿射频分复用（AFDM）传输方案，并推导相应的输入输出关系，在卫星接收端处理这些偏移。在块内信道不变的模型下，分离跨帧多普勒相位旋转，获得共享有效信道的多组观测。为表征分数时延和多普勒引起的离网泄漏，直接在多天线 AFDM 有效信道上学习基于得分的生成式先验，无须预设信道稀疏结构或要求路径参数与网格对齐。在此基础上构建 Turbo 消息传递接收机，对每个用户的完整信道张量进行联合去噪，并与线性估计模块交换外信息。信道去噪器采用有色高斯扰动进行预训练，以扰动协方差为条件；利用其条件均值特征训练活动分类器，并由条件均值的导数估计信道方差。所得接收机记为 C-STMP，在线保留标量方差消息，并根据后验预期残差能量更新查询方差。在已知跨帧相位的仿真中，C-STMP 在所评估信噪比下的信道估计误差和活动检测错误率均低于作为对照的基于得分的 Turbo 消息传递接收机。
